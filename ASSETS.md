# LUMÉ Asset Plan

## Temporary image groups
Use replaceable local assets or royalty-free stock/demo images for:
1. Hero hair transformation
2. Salon interior
3. Hair colour close-up
4. Stylist portraits (4)
5. Before image
6. After image
7. Styling detail
8. Product/detail shot
9. Reception/space
10. Gallery images (6–12)

## Asset rules
- Centralize all paths in one asset configuration file.
- Every image needs meaningful alt text unless decorative.
- Use `object-position` per image rather than distorting images.
- Provide mobile-friendly crops.
- Do not use AI-generated faces as if they were real staff.
- Temporary people must be clearly replaceable.

## Video
Hero video should be:
- 8–12 seconds
- muted
- looped
- compressed
- visually understandable without sound
- equipped with a poster image

## Logo
Primary logo is supplied as `assets/lume-logo.svg`.
