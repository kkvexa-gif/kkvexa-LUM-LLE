# LUMÉ Logo Direction

## Primary concept
A refined editorial wordmark: **LUMÉ** in a high-contrast serif with generous letter spacing.

## Construction
- Wordmark first; avoid a generic icon.
- Subtle gold hairline underneath.
- Strong black/ink version for light backgrounds.
- Ivory version for dark imagery.

## Clear space
Keep at least the height of the `É` around the wordmark.

## Do not
- Use generic beauty icons.
- Use scissors/comb icons as the main logo.
- Add excessive gold gradients.
- Put the logo inside a giant pill.
- Use ® or imply trademark registration.
