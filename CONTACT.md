## UX states
### Default
Clean labels with generous spacing.

### Focus
Visible accessible focus ring.

### Error
Inline message directly under the affected field.

### Loading
Button changes to `Sending…` and prevents duplicate submission.

### Success
Show:
> Thank you — your message has been received.

For demo mode, do not imply an actual email was delivered. A real email service can be connected later.
