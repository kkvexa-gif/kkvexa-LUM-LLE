# Product Requirements — LUMÉ

## 1. Vision
LUMÉ is a fictional premium salon brand used to demonstrate how a modern salon can present its work, artists, atmosphere and booking journey through a cinematic website.

The site should feel closer to a boutique fashion/beauty editorial experience than a SaaS landing page.

## 2. Target visitor
- First-time salon visitor
- Returning client
- Visitor comparing services and stylists
- Mobile visitor coming from Instagram or Google

## 3. Primary business goal
Drive visitors toward **Book Appointment** while giving enough visual and service information to build confidence.

## 4. Secondary goals
- Showcase salon atmosphere.
- Showcase stylist specialties.
- Explain services clearly.
- Present transformations.
- Provide location, opening hours and contact form.
- Make the brand memorable.

## 5. Required features
- Sticky responsive navigation.
- Desktop and mobile menu.
- Hero video/image sequence.
- Smooth section transitions.
- Scroll reveal animations.
- Services browsing/filtering interaction.
- Service detail modal or expandable detail.
- Before/after transformation slider.
- Stylist profiles.
- Gallery lightbox.
- About/story section.
- Google Maps location section.
- Contact form with Name, Email, Subject, Your message and Send button.
- Booking CTA repeated at logical points.
- Instagram/social placeholder links.
- Footer with contact, hours and navigation.
- Reduced-motion accessibility mode.
- Keyboard-accessible interactions.

## 6. Demo contact data
Use fictional/demo information and make it easy to replace:
- Email: hello@lume-demo.com
- Phone: +1 (555) 014-2026
- Location: Milton, Ontario, Canada
- Map query: Milton, Ontario, Canada
- Instagram: @lume.salon.demo

Do not present these as a real business's contact details.

## 7. Booking behavior
The demo can use a polished booking CTA that opens a booking panel or placeholder booking page. Do not integrate a real booking provider unless specifically requested later.

## 8. Content tone
Warm, confident, elegant and human. Avoid marketing clichés such as "revolutionary", "AI-powered", "world-class", "next-generation" and unsupported claims.
